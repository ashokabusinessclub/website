## About the event

The ABR Launch marks the release of each new issue of the Ashoka Business
Review. Writers present the arguments behind their pieces, editors talk through
the issue's theme, and the print run is distributed on the night.

## What happens

- Editorial introduction to the issue and its through-line
- Short readings and Q&A with contributing writers
- Distribution of the print edition
- Open floor for pitches for the following issue
