## What we do

Events & Operations is where plans meet deadlines. The department scopes each
event, builds the run-of-show, negotiates with vendors and runs the floor on the
day itself.

Flagships such as House of Cards and the ABR Launch are produced here, in close
coordination with Partnerships and Design.
