## What we do

Editorial owns the Ashoka Business Review end to end. We commission pieces,
work with writers over multiple drafts, and hold every argument to a sourcing
standard before publication.

Alongside long-form Publications, we run **Monocles** — short, sharp takes on a
single development, published quickly while it still matters.
