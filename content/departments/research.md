## What we do

Research & Strategy turns questions into defensible answers. Members pick a
sector or theme at the start of the semester, build a working thesis, and then
defend it in front of the department before anything is published.

Typical outputs include a quarterly sector primer, supporting data packs for the
Ashoka Business Review, and case decks used in club competitions.

## Who it suits

Students who enjoy digging through filings, datasets and interviews — regardless
of major. No prior finance background is required, only curiosity and follow-through.
