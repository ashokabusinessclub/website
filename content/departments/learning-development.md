This vertical focuses on creating meaningful learning opportunities that bridge curiosity with practical exposure across diverse business domains. Through interactive initiatives and engaging formats, the vertical aims to foster skill development, analytical thinking, and industry awareness in areas such as business affairs, consulting, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, product, and professional development. In addition, it will also work towards conceptualising larger flagship networking engagements that encourage insightful conversations and stronger industry interaction. This year, we are looking to re-structure the vertical's objectives to make it more aligned with the Industry Collaborations Vertical and potentially expand to mentoring and skill-building.
