ABR is the publishing wing of the Ashoka Business Club. It currently produces a bi-weekly newsletter, the Monocle, a semester-ly magazine, the Ashoka Business Review itself, and also maintains a website to showcase all the club's initiatives. This year, we aim to increase the credibility of our publications, through both, internal and external pieces of writing. Work within this vertical primarily involves writing, editing, peer-reviewing, and publishing articles across the appropriate platforms and formats. We also aim to clearly separate the responsibilities of the written publications team from those of the website team, given the different skill sets required for each. As a result, this vertical will focus mainly on the newsletter and magazine, rather than website management.
