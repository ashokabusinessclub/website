Finance acts as the engine of the Ashoka Business Club. It is tasked with raising funds for all club initiatives, managing the annual budget, and maintaining accurate accounts for the same. The work in this department involves ideating on deliverables, pitching to stakeholders, converting leads and maintaining clear accountability for the agreement to follow through. Since this vertical is fairly new, we are looking to bring in more structure and efficiency in over-all operations and expand its capacity in the coming year.
