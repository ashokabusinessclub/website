## About the event

Teams of four receive a real problem statement — often supplied by a partner
organisation — and have a fixed window to research, structure and present a
recommendation.

## Judging

Panels are drawn from consulting, venture and operating backgrounds. Teams are
scored on problem framing, evidence, feasibility and the quality of their
defence under questioning.
