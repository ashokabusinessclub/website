## About the series

Speaker Sessions are ABC's standing invitation to practitioners. Each session is
built around one guest and one question — a strategy call gone wrong, a founding
decision, a regulatory shift — rather than a general career talk.

## Format

Forty minutes of moderated conversation, twenty minutes of open student
questions, and an informal half hour afterwards. Sessions are student-moderated,
with prep briefs written by Research & Strategy.
