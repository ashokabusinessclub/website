## About the event

House of Cards is the Ashoka Business Club's signature simulation. Teams are
dealt a starting position and then spend the day negotiating, trading and
forming (and breaking) alliances under shifting market conditions.

## Format

- **Round 1 — Positioning.** Teams assess their holdings and set a strategy.
- **Round 2 — The floor.** Open negotiation, live pricing and public bids.
- **Round 3 — Shocks.** Injected events force rapid repricing and re-alliance.
- **Final.** Standings are settled and strategies are debriefed publicly.

## Why it matters

The simulation compresses a semester of strategy teaching into one day, and it
consistently draws the widest cross-section of the campus of anything we run.
