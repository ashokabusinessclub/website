The rate itself was the least interesting part of the announcement. The
statement's language shifted in two places, and both matter more than the
headline.

**First**, the description of inflation moved from "elevated" to "moderating but
uneven" — an acknowledgement that food and core are now telling different
stories.

**Second**, the forward guidance dropped its commitment phrasing entirely. That
is usually a prelude to optionality rather than to action.

Read together, the signal is patience, not pivot.
