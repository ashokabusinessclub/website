Equal founder splits are popular because they are easy to agree to and hard to
argue against in the first week. They are also the single most common source of
cap-table conflict by the second year.

The problem is not the number. It is that an equal split prices contribution
before contribution exists. Vesting schedules, role-based cliffs and documented
milestone reviews do more work than any starting percentage.

Decide the mechanism, not the symmetry.
