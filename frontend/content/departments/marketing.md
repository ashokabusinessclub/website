The Marketing Department of the Ashoka University Business Club plays a pivotal role in amplifying the club's visibility and impact across campus and beyond. It serves as the creative and communicative backbone for the entire club ecosystem. Its core function is to promote the initiatives and events organized by the other verticals whether that's designing collaterals for speaker series, creating social media campaigns for competitions, or crafting decks for internal use. This year, we are looking to use our socials to promote more endogenous activities of the club and all its verticals.
