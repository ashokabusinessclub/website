The Industry Collaborations Vertical works toward bridging the gap between students and industry. So far, it has done so by securing internships, organizing business trips and field visits, and facilitating hands-on practical projects. The work in this department involves outreach and partnership initiatives, managing industry relations, and executing opportunities that create tangible value for the student body. You are expected to engage with entrepreneurs, investors, and industry leaders to build long-term collaborations and expand the vertical's network and impact. This year, we are looking to re-structure the vertical's objectives to make it more cohesive with the Learning and Development Vertical and potentially shift from a field trip-heavy approach to a more high-paced Live Project approach.
