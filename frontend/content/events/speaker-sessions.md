## What they are

Closed-door, speaker-led sessions where members learn a real skill directly
from people doing the work, not from textbooks or case studies. Each session is
built to transfer a skill, not just deliver a talk.

## How they work

Every session follows a three-part build:

- **Input** — the speaker teaches the skill and the thinking behind it.
- **Application** — the room applies it live, working through a real problem with the speaker.
- **Tie-up** — open Q&A and a real-world teardown that ties it all together.

## What they help with

- Learning practical, job-ready skills you can use the same week
- Closing the gap between a liberal-arts education and industry expectations
- Direct access to founders, operators, and analysts actually in the field
- Clarity on how people break into finance, consulting, entrepreneurship, and beyond

## What we're aiming for

- Sessions with a sharp, specific focus, each teaching one clear, usable skill
- A recurring, recognisable series rather than scattered one-off talks
- Credible industry voices matched to what students actually want to learn
- Formats that keep the room active: live application, real cases, honest Q&A
