## About the event

House of Cards is a high-intensity corporate crisis simulation hosted by
Ashoka Business Club, in partnership with the InfoEdge Centre for
Entrepreneurship.

Top student teams tackle real-world business challenges under time-bound
pressure. Unlike conventional case competitions, the event revolves around one
exclusive industry partner — all problem statements and simulations are drawn
from that company's actual challenges.

Participants work not just for certificates, but for the chance to build
tangible solutions and engage directly with the partner organisation.

## Rounds

**Qualifier Round.** The competition began with an online fast-paced quiz
designed to test participants on business fundamentals, industry awareness,
analytical thinking, and current affairs. The top 30 teams advanced to the next
stage of the competition.

**Mini Case + Presentation Round.** Shortlisted teams analysed a time-bound
mini-case centred on Australia's automotive innovation ecosystem, identifying
the perception gap and evaluating the country's position as a future mobility
destination. Teams then presented their recommendations to a panel, defending
assumptions, strategic choices, and implementation roadmap.

**Corporate Crisis Round (BMW Showroom).** A high-stakes corporate crisis
simulation designed to test strategic thinking, leadership, and
decision-making under pressure. Teams responded to a fast-evolving business
crisis in the automotive industry and defended their decisions before industry
professionals and faculty.
